### Defining the Social Determinants of Health and the Capabilities Approach

To avoid the textbook defintions we need to first define the social-determinants of health in broad terms with respect to the capabilities approach, and we can begin by infering that Social Determinants are ultimately correlated to what makes society ultimately function under a democarcy and the capability approach simply states that these conventional labels that make society function do not always respect the defintion of specificty; With the scoial determinants of health in relation to the capiablites approach being defined in broad terms we can then begin to define them more specefically, and instead of going in depth we can examine there relation to another with respect to the data science process. To elaborate on this thought we can define the social determinants broadly as econmic stability, healthcare access / quality, education access / quality, social / community context, and neighborhood / built enviroment, yet we must consider that the recognized broad defintion can be subjective due to the potentional overlapping sub-categories that can be grouped within broader ones. 

### Articulate how these two concepts are connected and contribute to livelihoods or well-being.

With respect to data science, building datasets with overlapping sub-categories can result in correlations that may not always be extremly relevant and can be possibly interpreted by a lack of causation. A good example would be the relationship between econmic stability and Education access and quality, and by diving deeper we can find that poverty is generally associated with econmic stability, yet we can argue that poverty overlaps with ones access to education and does influence ones quality of education that they may recieve; Escpically at the college level since higher education is not mandated by the state. We can apply what we now know to the world around us and ask the question what percentage of individuals that have lived below the recognized econmic threshold for poverty for more than 60% of there life complete higher education? This is a very interesting question that from logic would present its self as poverty being an indicator that the individual is at risk of having to take on life early and possibly work instead of being able to pursue a higher education, and then this would lead us to infer that not getting a degree does not disqualify you as being inteligent or successull (people commonly combine these concepts), therefore now we would just need a solid dataset to answer this question.
